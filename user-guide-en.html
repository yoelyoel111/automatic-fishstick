💡 Tips and Tricks

How to get the most out of the site

🎯 For Best Results

✓ Use high quality images - At least 300 DPI for professional printing
✓ Calibrate your printer - For maximum precision in measurements
✓ Save projects - For faster work in the future
✓ Use print preview - Before printing

⚠️ Things to Avoid

✗ Low resolution images - Will cause blurring in print
✗ Too small margins - May get cut off in printing
✗ Too light colors - Won't print well
✗ Too small text - Won't be readable
